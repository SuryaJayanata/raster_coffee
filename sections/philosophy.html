The Grid as a Method of Extraction

In 1961, Swiss graphic designer Josef Müller-Brockmann formulated the grid system as an instrument of clarity, order, and intellectual honesty. At Raster, we apply this exact discipline to coffee extraction: eliminating randomness, calibrating every particle size, and balancing mineral dissolved solids.

Pillar 01 · Chemistry

Custom Remineralized Water

Coffee is 98.6% water. Our lab utilizes dual-stage reverse osmosis remineralized to an exact 128 ppm TDS, with a 2:1 magnesium-to-calcium ratio to amplify natural fruit sweetness without harsh astringency.

Target Buffer: 45 ppm CaCO3 pH 7.1 Neutral
Pillar 02 · Geometry

Bimodal Particle Calibration

Equipped with 98mm SSP Red Speed burrs, we test grind distribution curves daily using laser diffraction models. This minimizes unwanted fines and delivers crystal-clear flavor separation on filter cups.

Micron Spread: 450 - 680 µm Fines < 4.8%
Pillar 03 · Origin

Ethical Direct Trade

We purchase green beans directly from generational farmer cooperatives in Sumatra, Java, Sulawesi, and Bali. We pay an average of 42% above fair trade minimums to guarantee living wages and soil conservation.

Annual Transparency Index 100% Traceable
