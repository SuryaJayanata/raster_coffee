About

Born from the geometry of order, roasted for pure clarity.

Raster was founded in Tlogomas, Malang—a vibrant neighborhood where student energy, cool mountain breezes, and East Java’s rich coffee roasting traditions converge. Taking inspiration from the Swiss International Typographic Style and Josef Müller-Brockmann’s grid philosophy, we set out to merge our local volcanic coffee roots with uncompromising architectural discipline.

Surrounded by the terroirs of Mount Arjuno, Bromo, and Dampit, we work side-by-side with generational farming families across East Java and the Indonesian archipelago. By controlling extraction ratios, water mineralization, and drum roaster heat curves to millimeter precision, we eliminate harsh bitterness and unveil clean, structured, sweet fruit profiles.

Every pour is an honest reflection of highland craft, deliberate restraint, and the creative spirit of Tlogomas, Malang.
